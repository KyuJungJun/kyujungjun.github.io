## Spring 2026 (Korea University)

### Engineering Mathematics II (MOBI 204) · 공업수학 II
**Schedule:** Tue/Thu, 10:30–12:00  
This course continues from Engineering Mathematics I and covers vector differential and integral calculus, Fourier series/transform, and core topics in linear algebra.

### Fundamentals of Energy Systems (MOBI 108) · 에너지 시스템의 이해
**Schedule:** Tue/Thu, 12:00–13:30  
An introduction to energy conversion and storage systems, combining fundamental knowledge of thermodynamics, electrochemistry, and heat transfer, together with guest seminars and a team project analyzing a real-world energy technology.
