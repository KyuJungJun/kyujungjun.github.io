---
title: Ion transport mechanisms in superionic conductors
order: 3
question: Why do some crystal structures conduct Li and Na ions orders of magnitude faster than others?
methods:
  - Ab initio and machine-learning molecular dynamics
  - Algorithms that detect correlated ion hops and anion-group motion
  - Structure–property analysis across material families
papers:
  - 10.1038/s41563-022-01222-4
  - 10.1073/pnas.2316493121
  - Jun2024DiffusionMechanisms
  - 10.1016/j.matt.2025.102001
  - 10.1073/pnas.2423194122
---

We study how the framework of a crystal (corner-sharing polyhedra, anion-group rotation and tilting, van der Waals gaps) sets the energy landscape for ion hops, and turn these findings into design rules for new solid electrolytes.
