question: How can simulations keep first-principles accuracy while reaching nanosecond time scales and nanometer length scales?

  - Generative models for accelerated dynamics

We develop, fine-tune, and test machine-learning interatomic potentials, and use generative models to accelerate molecular dynamics, so that transport in disordered and complex materials can be simulated directly.
